CompassLive (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides a digital compass application for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, collect, use, and share when you use CompassLive.

By using CompassLive, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information you provide directly

CompassLive does not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, postal address, or payment details through the app itself.

b. Device and app information

When you use CompassLive, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the app and by third-party services used in the app, including:

  • device model
  • operating system version
  • app version
  • language and locale
  • IP address
  • advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, where available
  • basic diagnostic, performance, and ad-related information

c. Location information

CompassLive may access your device’s precise location only when you enable features that require it, such as True North calculation and map-related functionality.

Precise location is used to help the app determine magnetic declination and support compass-related features on your device. We do not use your precise location to build a user profile, and we do not sell your precise location information.

Based on the current app design, precise location used for compass functionality is processed locally on the device. However, third-party advertising services used in the app may process device and network information, including IP address and other identifiers, for advertising, fraud prevention, and measurement purposes, subject to their own policies.

2. Permissions We Request

CompassLive may request the following permissions:

Precise Location

Used only if you choose to enable features that rely on location, such as True North and opening map-related functionality.

You can deny this permission and continue using the app’s basic compass functionality, although some features may be limited.

Internet / Network Access

Used to load advertising and support normal app connectivity.

Advertising ID

Used by advertising services, including Google AdMob, to support ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement, in accordance with applicable platform rules and Google’s policies.
